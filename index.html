📖 HOW TO PLAY PAPER WAR

  1. Step 1: Secret Placement — Both players place their 10 soldiers anywhere on their half of the notebook sheet. Opponent positions are kept hidden.
  2. Step 2: Rock-Paper-Scissors — Quick showdown to decide who gets the first strike.
  3. Step 3: The Blind Strike — On your turn, mark a dot with your pen on your own side of the paper where you predict the mirrored enemy soldier is hiding.
  4. Step 4: The 3D Fold — The paper physically lifts, curves, and slaps down on the crease! If your mirrored dot touches an enemy soldier, that soldier is killed with an ink splatter.
  5. Step 5: Historical Smudges — Missed shots leave smudges on the paper, giving you clues to adjust your aim!
  6. Victory — The first player to eliminate all 10 enemy soldiers wins the war.
